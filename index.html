Session notes

Safety and progression

  • Do not train through increasing pain, new swelling, locking or actual giving-way.
  • If symptoms are worse the next morning, reduce or pause the provoking activity and discuss it with your physiotherapist.
  • Eyes-closed single-leg balance is an advanced drill; only do it with the safe setup and supervision advised by your physiotherapist.
  • Return to running only after clinical clearance and a gradual run-walk progression.
